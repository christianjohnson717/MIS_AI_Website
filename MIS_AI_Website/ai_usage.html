Correct AI Usage

My Thoughts:
I think AI is one of the best tools students can use for studying if they treat it like a personal tutor.
It can explain something in a different way if I do not understand it the first time.
I also think it is helpful for making practice questions and checking what I know.
Students should use it to learn, not to cheat or have it do all their work for them.

ChatGPT's Feedback:
I agree that using AI as a personal tutor can be a helpful way to study. Asking for explanations and practice questions supports learning instead of replacing it. One thing I would add is that AI can make mistakes, so students should check important answers and follow their professor's rules about when AI is allowed.

Benefits of AI

  1. Explains difficult topics in a way that is easier to understand.
  2. Helps students study with practice questions and examples.

Who Benefits?

  1. College students who need extra help studying.
  2. Employees who want to learn or work more efficiently.

How AI Tools Used

  • Asking AI to explain a confusing class topic.
  • Making a study guide to review before an exam.

Incorrect AI Usage

Cheating on Assignments
Submitting answers written by AI as your own work when your professor does not allow it.
Not Checking Information
Copying AI answers without checking if the information is actually correct.
Using AI During Tests
Using AI to answer exam questions when outside help is not permitted.

I think AI is a great tool, but it becomes a problem when students use it to avoid doing their work. It is wrong to turn in AI answers as your own when it is not allowed. Students also miss out on learning when they let AI do everything for them. I think the best way to use AI is responsibly, like a tutor instead of a shortcut.
