Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

One of the most famous cybersecurity incidents in recent history was the WannaCry ransomware attack, which began on May 12, 2017. The malware spread rapidly across computer networks by exploiting a vulnerability in Microsoft Windows systems. It encrypted victims' files and demanded ransom payments in Bitcoin in exchange for the possibility of recovering their data. The attack disrupted hospitals, businesses, and organizations in more than 150 countries, including parts of the United Kingdom's National Health Service. WannaCry became a major lesson in the importance of installing security updates, maintaining backups, and protecting computer networks from known vulnerabilities.

Learn more about the attack from the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) .


Types of Malware

  1. Ransomware: Malicious software that locks or encrypts data and demands payment.
  2. Trojan Horse: Malicious software that disguises itself as a legitimate program to trick users.

Common Hacker Motives

  1. Financial Gain: Stealing money, financial information, or valuable business data.
  2. Political Activism: Attacking systems to promote a political cause or draw attention to an issue.

Prevention Strategies

  • Multi-Factor Authentication: Require an additional verification step when signing in.
  • Firewalls and Security Updates: Monitor network traffic and patch known software vulnerabilities.
